01

The central question

Detecting a conflict is only
the beginning.

A rule can expose an inconsistency. Choosing a correction requires something more: evidence that applies to the right record, attribute and task.

This survey follows that connection from knowledge acquisition to repair selection, then into multi-step data preparation. It distinguishes factual correctness, constraint consistency and downstream task utility.

Take home

what changes.

Every task should make the next one smarter. Reuse knowledge with the evidence that makes it trustworthy.

Act→Validate→Remember
  1. 01

    Remember what you verify

    Retain verified rules, meanings and experience for later decisions.

  2. 02

    Update with evidence

    Revise or retire knowledge when its conditions no longer hold.

  3. 03

    Ask when evidence is missing

    Inspect the data, retrieve context or ask the user.

  4. 04

    Carry knowledge across tasks

    Connect cleaning, transformation and analysis.

  5. 05

    Verify every step
